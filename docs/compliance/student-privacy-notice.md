## 1. Full notice (for Student Portal and Debt Office website/signage)

> **How your financial data is used in automated debt management**
>
> As a registered student, your financial and funding information is used by an
> automated system to help identify students who may need support managing their
> student account, and to recommend appropriate next steps to a debt officer.
>
> **What data is used**
> We use information already held in your student record, including:
> - Your outstanding balance and how long amounts have been owing
> - Your funding source and funding status (e.g. self-funded, NSFAS, bursary)
> - Your payment history, including missed instalments and any existing payment
>   arrangement
> - Whether you have an active credit case on your account
>
> **How it's used**
> Each night, this information is used to calculate a risk indicator for your
> account. If your account is flagged, a debt officer reviews your case and a
> system-generated, policy-based recommendation for how to proceed — this could
> include a payment reminder, an offer to set up a payment arrangement, or, in more
> serious cases, escalation. **A human debt officer always reviews and decides on
> any action before it is taken** — the system does not act on your account without
> officer approval, except for standard low-risk reminders.
> Register holds and refunds are never actioned automatically, at any stage.
>
> **How long your data is kept**
> Records related to this process are kept for 7 years after the end of your final
> financial year with the institution, in line with SARS and audit-retention
> requirements, after which they are permanently deleted.
>
> **Who can see it**
> Only debt officers assigned to your case (or covering for your officer), and
> compliance/audit staff reviewing the audit trail, can see this information. Access
> is limited to what each person needs for their role.
>
> **Your rights**
> You have the right to:
> - **Ask what data we hold** about you and how your risk indicator was calculated
> - **Request a copy** of your case record and the recommendation history on your
>   account
> - **Dispute a recommendation or action** you believe is incorrect, by contacting
>   the Debt Management Office (details below) — a human will review your case
> - **Query or object** to this processing through the institution's existing
>   POPIA/PAIA request channels
>
> This process does not replace or limit any of your existing rights to query your
> student account through normal Debtors/Fees channels.
>
> **Questions or concerns**
> Contact the Debt Management Office: _[contact details — email/phone/office
> location to be filled in by Registrar's Office]_
> For formal data-protection queries, contact the Institution's Information Officer:
> _[contact details — to be filled in by Registrar's Office]_

## 2. Short notice (for registration privacy notice insert)

> Your financial and funding data may be used by an automated system to identify
> possible payment risk and recommend support options; a human debt officer always
> reviews and approves any action taken. Records are kept for 7 years after your
> final financial year. See [Student Portal / debt office website link] for full
> details and your rights, including how to query or dispute a recommendation.
